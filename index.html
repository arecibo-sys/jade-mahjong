Jade Mahjong

Make four sets and a pair. A chow is 3 consecutive suit tiles; a pung is 3 matching tiles; a kong is 4 matching tiles. Tap a tile, then discard it.

You are East and open the round. Claim an opponent’s discard with the action bar. Sound can be toggled any time.
